Keystone Hardscapes Services Overview

About Keystone Hardscapes

Keystone Hardscapes is a premier landscaping company based in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. We specialize in transforming outdoor spaces into beautiful, functional living areas. With over 5 years of experience in the landscaping industry, our team is dedicated to delivering exceptional quality and customer service.

Core Services

Patio Design and Installation

We design and install custom patios using a variety of materials including natural stone, pavers, and decorative concrete. Our patio services include:

- Custom design consultation to match your vision and property
- Site preparation and proper drainage installation
- Installation of pavers, flagstone, or stamped concrete
- Sealing and finishing for long-lasting beauty
- Integration with existing landscape features

Typical patio projects range from simple 200 sq ft sitting areas to expansive 1000+ sq ft outdoor living spaces. We can incorporate built-in seating, fire pits, and outdoor kitchen areas.

Retaining Walls

Our retaining wall services address both functional and aesthetic needs:

- Terracing for sloped properties
- Erosion control and soil retention
- Garden bed separation and definition
- Decorative landscape features
- Engineered solutions for large height differences

We use a variety of materials including:
- Natural stone (limestone, sandstone)
- Concrete blocks and Allan blocks
- Boulder walls for natural look
- Timber walls for certain applications

Retaining walls are engineered for stability and drainage. All walls over 4 feet require engineering certification, which we can arrange. Pricing starts at approximately $45 per square foot depending on materials and site conditions.

Outdoor Kitchens and Living Spaces

Transform your backyard into an entertainment hub with our outdoor kitchen services:

- Custom BBQ islands with built-in grills
- Countertops in granite, concrete, or stone
- Storage cabinets and drawers (weather-resistant)
- Refrigeration units and beverage centers
- Sinks with proper drainage
- Covered structures and pergolas
- Outdoor bars and serving areas

We work with top brands for appliances and can customize the design to match your home's architecture and your cooking preferences.

Fire Pits and Features

Add warmth and ambiance with our fire feature installations:

- Built-in stone fire pits (wood-burning or gas)
- Portable fire pit installations
- Linear fire features
- Fire tables
- Proper seating area integration
- Gas line installation (certified technicians)

All fire features are installed according to local fire codes and safety regulations.

Concrete Restoration and Decorative Concrete

Specialized concrete services include:

- Stamped concrete patios and walkways
- Exposed aggregate finishes
- Concrete resurfacing and repair
- Decorative concrete overlays
- Concrete sealing and protection
- Color and pattern customization

Concrete is a durable, cost-effective option for large areas and can be designed to mimic natural stone at a fraction of the cost.

Snow Removal Services

Winter services for residential and commercial properties:

- Driveway and walkway clearing
- Parking lot snow removal
- Ice management and salting
- Seasonal contracts available
- Emergency storm response
- Commercial property maintenance
