Surrounding Areas

For projects outside our standard service area (within 50km of Calgary), please contact us. We evaluate each project individually and are often willing to travel for:

- Large projects (over $15,000)
- Unique or interesting projects
- Referrals from existing customers
- Multi-phase projects

Travel fees for extended areas are determined by:
- Distance from Calgary
- Project size and value
- Access and site conditions
- Duration of project

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you charge travel fees within Calgary?
A: No, there are no travel fees for any projects within Calgary city limits.

Q: What about Airdrie, Cochrane, or Okotoks?
A: No travel fees for our primary service area communities.

Q: I'm in Bragg Creek, can you help?
A: Yes, we can work in Bragg Creek. There may be a travel fee depending on project size. Projects over $10,000 typically include travel costs.

Q: Do you work outside the listed areas?
A: Possibly. Contact us with your location and project details. We evaluate each request individually.

Q: Why don't you service Canmore or Banff?
A: The distance makes it difficult to provide our standard level of service and warranty support. Travel time would also significantly increase project costs.

Q: Can you do one-day projects outside your service area?
A: For very small projects, travel time may make it not cost-effective. We focus on areas where we can provide the best value.

Q: I'm buying a new home in [location], can you do the landscaping?
A: Contact us with the specific location and we'll let you know if it's in our service area.
