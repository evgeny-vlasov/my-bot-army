Keystone Hardscapes Warranty Information

Our Commitment to Quality

At Keystone Hardscapes, we stand behind our work with one of the most comprehensive warranties in the Calgary landscaping industry. Every project we complete comes with a 5-year warranty on both materials and workmanship.

5-Year Comprehensive Warranty

Coverage Period: 5 full years from project completion date

What's Covered:

1. Structural Integrity
   - Retaining walls stability and alignment
   - Patio levelness and base integrity
   - Proper settling (beyond normal seasonal movement)
   - Foundation and base work
   - Structural components of outdoor kitchens
   - Fire pit construction integrity

2. Material Defects
   - Manufacturing defects in pavers, blocks, or stone
   - Premature deterioration of materials
   - Color fading beyond normal weathering
   - Cracking or breaking of properly maintained materials
   - Sealant failures (when applied by us)

3. Workmanship Quality
   - Installation errors or improper techniques
   - Improper drainage causing pooling or erosion
   - Joint sand washout (beyond normal maintenance)
   - Improper compaction leading to settling
   - Installation not meeting industry standards

4. Drainage Function
   - Drainage systems we installed
   - Proper water flow and management
   - Prevention of water damage to structures
   - French drain functionality (if installed)

What's NOT Covered:

Normal Wear and Tear
- Expected weathering and aging
- Normal seasonal expansion/contraction
- Surface patina development on natural materials
- Fading from UV exposure (within normal parameters)

Acts of Nature
- Damage from extreme weather events
- Flooding beyond design specifications
- Earthquake or ground shift damage
- Tree root damage from existing trees
- Frost heave beyond normal expectations

Lack of Maintenance
- Efflorescence not cleaned promptly
- Weed growth in joints (requires periodic maintenance)
- Stains from spills not cleaned promptly
- Mold/mildew growth (requires periodic cleaning)
- Deterioration from neglect

Misuse or Damage
- Damage from vehicles driving on patios/walkways
- Damage from snow plowing or snow blowers
- Intentional damage or vandalism
- Modifications by others
- Chemical damage from de-icers or harsh cleaners

Third Party Work
- Damage caused by other contractors
- Utility work that disturbs our installation
- Landscaping changes that affect drainage
- Building additions that change water flow

Exclusions and Limitations

1. Material Coverage
   - Materials are warranted by their manufacturers
   - We honor all manufacturer warranties
   - Some materials have different warranty periods
   - Premium materials may have extended coverage

2. Living Materials
   - Plants, sod, and trees have a 1-year warranty (not 5 years)
   - Requires proper watering and care
   - Subject to seasonal planting guarantees

3. Seasonal Limitations
   - Warranty work scheduled based on weather
   - Some repairs only possible in dry, warm weather
   - Emergency issues addressed promptly
   - Non-urgent items scheduled appropriately

4. Payment Required
   - Warranty is void if final payment not received
   - Must be in good standing on payment terms
   - No warranty coverage for unpaid work

Maintenance for Warranty Protection

To keep your warranty valid, follow these guidelines:

Regular Cleaning
- Sweep patios and walkways regularly
- Clean spills promptly
- Power wash annually (on appropriate setting)
- Remove leaves and debris

Weed Prevention
- Keep polymeric sand joints intact
- Apply joint stabilizing sand as needed
- Address weeds promptly
- Consider annual re-sanding

Winter Care
- Use only ice melt safe for pavers/concrete
- Avoid metal shovels (use plastic)
- Don't pile snow against walls
- Monitor for ice dams

Drainage Maintenance
- Keep drainage pathways clear
- Don't block drainage holes
- Monitor for proper water flow
- Clean out catch basins if installed

Proper Use
- Avoid vehicles on patios
- Don't use harsh chemicals
- Follow care instructions provided
- Report issues promptly
