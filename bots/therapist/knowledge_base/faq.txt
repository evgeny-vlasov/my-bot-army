FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS - VALERY BELYANIN PSYCHOTHERAPY
========================================

PAYMENT QUESTIONS
========================================

No. Credit cards are NOT accepted at this time.

No. Credit cards are not available as a payment method.

INSURANCE QUESTIONS
========================================

Do you take insurance?
Valery accepts Canadian extended health benefits. You pay the session fee and submit your receipt for reimbursement. Many employer benefit plans cover psychotherapy.

Do I need a referral?
No. You do not need a referral from your family doctor to see a psychotherapist. You can book directly.

SESSION QUESTIONS
========================================

How long is a session?
Standard sessions are 50 minutes plus 10 minutes for summing up. Extended sessions of up to 90 minutes are available on a prorated basis.

How often should I come?
Weekly sessions are the common starting point. Frequency can decrease to bi-weekly or monthly as you improve. It's up to you.

What if I miss a session?
Please try to reschedule beforehand. You may be charged for missed appointments without advance notice.

ABOUT THE THERAPIST
========================================

What are Valery's credentials?
Valery Belyanin is a Registered Psychotherapist with the College of Registered Psychotherapists of Ontario (CRPO), a Certified Mental Health Professional (OAMHP), and holds PhDs in Linguistics, Psycholinguistics, and Psychology of Personality.

Where is the practice located?
Valery is based in Toronto (M4C), Ontario, Canada, but primarily works online with clients worldwide.

What languages do you offer?
Sessions are available in English and Russian.

THERAPY QUESTIONS
========================================

What therapy approaches do you use?
Valery uses evidence-based approaches including Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy (REBT), Cognitive Behavioral Coaching, Supportive psychotherapy, and mindfulness-based approaches.

What do you specialize in?
Valery specializes in anxiety, depression, OCD, relationship issues, and stress management.

Can you prescribe medication?
No. Psychotherapists do not prescribe medication in Canada. Only psychiatrists (medical doctors) can prescribe medication.

Can you write a report for my lawyer?
Yes. Valery can write reports for legal representatives after a certain number of sessions. This is available for criminal offense cases, refugee status claims, and other legal matters.
