INSURANCE, FEES, AND PAYMENT - VALERY BELYANIN PSYCHOTHERAPY
========================================

EXTENDED HEALTH BENEFITS (CANADA)
========================================

Valery Belyanin accepts extended health benefits for psychotherapy services.

How Extended Health Benefits Work:
- Many Canadian employers provide extended health benefits that cover psychotherapy
- Coverage varies by plan - typically 50-100% up to an annual maximum
- You pay the session fee and submit your receipt for reimbursement
- Some plans offer direct billing; check with your provider

Before Your First Session:
- Contact your benefits provider to verify psychotherapy coverage
- Ask about your annual maximum and per-session limits
- Confirm if Registered Psychotherapists (RP) are covered
- Check if you need a referral (typically not required for psychotherapy)

Electronic Receipts:
- Electronic receipts are issued after each session
- Submit receipts to your insurance company for reimbursement
- Some clients with businesses claim psychotherapy as consulting services for tax purposes

========================================
SESSION INFORMATION
========================================

Standard Session: 50 minutes (plus 10 minutes for summing up)
Extended Session: Up to 90 minutes, prorated based on standard rates

Session Frequency:
- Weekly sessions are the common starting point
- Helps build momentum, trust, and address acute issues
- Frequency can decrease to bi-weekly or monthly as you improve
- Ultimately, session frequency is up to you

Missed Sessions:
- Please try to reschedule sessions beforehand
- You may be charged for missed appointments without advance notice

REFERRALS

Do I need a referral from my family doctor?
- A referral is usually required for medical consultations with a psychiatrist
- Since Valery Belyanin is a psychotherapist (not a psychiatrist), NO referral is needed
