INSURANCE AND FEE INFORMATION - PSYLING

At Psyling, we believe that quality mental health care should be accessible. This document outlines our fee structure, insurance policies, and payment options.

========================================
STANDARD SESSION FEES
========================================

Our fee structure is competitive with other private practice therapy services in the area:

Individual Therapy Sessions (50 minutes): $150 per session
Couples Therapy Sessions (60-75 minutes): $180 per session
Initial Consultation/Intake (60 minutes): $175

Extended Sessions:
Some clients benefit from longer sessions. Extended session fees are prorated based on standard rates.

========================================
INSURANCE
========================================

We are in-network providers for the following insurance plans:
- Blue Cross Blue Shield (all plans)
- Aetna
- Cigna
- United Healthcare / UnitedHealthcare
- Medicare

We are out-of-network for other insurance plans, but many clients can still receive partial reimbursement through their out-of-network benefits.

What "In-Network" Means:
- We bill your insurance directly
- You pay your copay or coinsurance at each session
- Your insurance covers the remaining balance
- Deductibles may apply depending on your plan

What "Out-of-Network" Means:
- You pay the full session fee at the time of service
- We provide you with a detailed receipt (superbill)
- You submit the superbill to your insurance for reimbursement
- Many plans reimburse 50-80% of out-of-network services
- Reimbursement depends on your specific plan benefits

Verifying Your Benefits:
We recommend calling your insurance provider before your first session to verify:
- Your mental health coverage and copay
- Your deductible and whether it's been met
- Number of sessions covered per year
- Whether pre-authorization is required

Our staff is happy to help you understand your benefits, but we encourage you to verify details directly with your insurance company.

========================================
PAYMENT OPTIONS
========================================

We accept the following forms of payment:
- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover)
- Debit cards
- HSA/FSA cards
- Checks
- Cash

Payment is due at the time of service unless other arrangements have been made.

We keep a credit card on file for:
- Session fees
- Cancelled or missed appointments (see cancellation policy)
- Any remaining balance after insurance processing

========================================
SLIDING SCALE
========================================

We believe everyone deserves access to quality mental health care. We reserve a limited number of sliding scale spots for clients experiencing financial hardship.

Sliding Scale Fees:
- Available based on financial need
- Requires documentation of income
- Rates typically range from $80-$120 per session
- Priority given to clients without insurance coverage
- Subject to availability

To inquire about sliding scale options:
- Contact our office to discuss your situation
- We'll review your financial circumstances confidentially
- Sliding scale agreements are reviewed periodically

========================================
GOOD FAITH ESTIMATE
========================================

Under the No Surprises Act, you have the right to receive a "Good Faith Estimate" of expected charges for therapy services.

What This Means:
- We will provide an estimate of expected costs
- Estimate covers services over a reasonable timeframe
- Actual costs may vary based on your specific treatment needs
- You'll receive an updated estimate if treatment plans change significantly

To request a Good Faith Estimate:
Ask our administrative staff, and we'll provide this within the timeframe required by law.

========================================
CANCELLATION POLICY
========================================

We understand that unexpected situations arise. Please note our cancellation policy:

Notice Required:
- 24 hours advance notice required to cancel or reschedule
- You can cancel by phone, email, or through our client portal
- Cancellations made with less than 24 hours notice are charged the full session fee

Late Cancellation Fees:
- Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice: Full session fee
- No-shows (did not cancel): Full session fee
- Insurance does not cover late cancellation or no-show fees

Exceptions:
- Emergencies (illness, family crisis, etc.) are handled on a case-by-case basis
- Please communicate with your therapist about extenuating circumstances

Why This Policy Exists:
- Your appointment time is reserved exclusively for you
- Late cancellations prevent others from accessing that time slot
- Therapists' availability is limited and time is valuable

========================================
SUPERBILLS FOR OUT-OF-NETWORK CLIENTS
========================================

If you're using out-of-network benefits or paying privately and seeking reimbursement, we provide detailed superbills.

What's Included:
- Provider information (name, credentials, NPI number)
- Your information
- Dates of service
- CPT codes (procedure codes for therapy services)
- Diagnosis codes (ICD-10 codes)
- Amount paid
- Practice tax ID

How to Use Your Superbill:
1. Receive superbill after each session (or monthly)
2. Submit to your insurance company
3. Insurance reimburses you directly based on your out-of-network benefits
4. Keep records of all superbills and reimbursements for taxes

We typically provide superbills:
- After each session
- Monthly via email
- Available through client portal

========================================
FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE RESOURCES
========================================

If cost is a barrier to accessing care, here are some resources:

1. Sliding Scale: As mentioned above, we offer limited sliding scale appointments

2. Employee Assistance Programs (EAP):
   - Many employers offer free counseling sessions through EAP
   - Check with your HR department
   - We can work with many EAP providers

3. Community Mental Health Centers:
   - Offer low-cost or free services based on income
   - May have longer wait times

4. University Training Clinics:
   - Reduced fees for therapy provided by supervised graduate students
   - Quality care with experienced supervision

5. Open Path Collective:
   - National network offering reduced-fee therapy ($30-$80/session)
   - www.openpathcollective.org

6. SAMHSA Treatment Locator:
   - Free service to find local mental health resources
   - www.samhsa.gov or call 1-800-662-4357

We're committed to helping you access the care you need, regardless of your financial situation. Please don't hesitate to discuss concerns about cost.
