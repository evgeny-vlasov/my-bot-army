========================================
WHAT TO EXPECT IN YOUR FIRST SESSION
========================================

Initial sessions typically involve:
- Getting to know each other
- Discussing what brought you to therapy
- Exploring your goals
- Obtaining informed consent
- Creating a safe, confidential space for conversation

Many clients experience some relief even after the first conversation.

========================================
SESSION DETAILS
========================================

Duration:
- Standard session: 50 minutes (plus 10 minutes for summing up)
- Extended sessions: Up to 90 minutes, available on a prorated basis

Frequency:
- Weekly sessions are the common starting point
- Helps build momentum, trust, and address acute issues like depression or anxiety
- Frequency often decreases to bi-weekly or monthly as you improve
- Session frequency is ultimately your choice - there is no pressure

Platform:
- Sessions are conducted via the secure Psychology Today Online Platform
- No program installation needed
- Alternative platforms available: WhatsApp, Telegram

========================================
DO I NEED PSYCHOTHERAPY?
========================================

People seek psychotherapy for many reasons:
- Dealing with specific challenges (anxiety, depression, stress)
- Personal growth and self-understanding
- Relationship issues
- Mental health concerns
- Life transitions
- Trauma processing

Seeking psychotherapy is a positive step. If you're wondering whether it's right for you, scheduling a session with Valery is the best way to explore that question.

========================================
HOW LONG DOES PSYCHOTHERAPY TAKE?
========================================

Therapy duration varies based on individual needs and goals:
- Short-term focused work: 8-12 sessions
- Longer-term therapy: For deeper issues or ongoing support
- There is no pressure from Valery regarding duration

The pace and length of therapy is discussed together and adjusted to your needs.

========================================
COMMON CONCERNS
========================================

"I'm feeling anxious/depressed/overwhelmed..."
These feelings are difficult, and seeking support is a positive step. Psychotherapy can help you develop coping strategies and address underlying issues. Consider scheduling a session to discuss your situation.

"Can you help me with [specific problem]?"
Psychotherapy can help with many issues. Valery can provide a personalized assessment during a session and discuss how therapy might address your specific concerns.

"What if I miss a session?"
Please try to reschedule sessions beforehand. You may be charged for missed appointments without advance notice.
