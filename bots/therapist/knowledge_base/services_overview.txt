PSYLING THERAPY SERVICES

Psyling offers comprehensive mental health services to individuals, couples, and families seeking support for a wide range of concerns.

========================================
INDIVIDUAL THERAPY
========================================

We provide one-on-one therapy sessions tailored to each client's unique needs and goals. Our therapists are trained in evidence-based approaches and work collaboratively with clients to develop effective treatment plans.

Common Reasons People Seek Individual Therapy:
- Anxiety and panic disorders
- Depression and mood disorders
- Life transitions (career changes, relocation, major life events)
- Stress management and burnout
- Grief and loss
- Trauma and PTSD
- Self-esteem and identity issues
- Relationship difficulties
- Personal growth and self-discovery

Session Format:
- Typical sessions are 50 minutes
- Frequency is usually weekly, but can be adjusted based on needs
- Sessions are conducted in a private, confidential setting
- Both in-person and telehealth options available

========================================
COUPLES THERAPY
========================================

We help couples strengthen their relationships, improve communication, and work through challenges together. Our approach is grounded in Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT) and focuses on understanding attachment patterns and emotional needs.

Common Reasons Couples Seek Therapy:
- Communication problems
- Conflict resolution difficulties
- Trust and infidelity issues
- Intimacy concerns
- Life transitions and adjustments
- Premarital counseling
- Parenting disagreements
- Considering separation or working on reconciliation

Session Format:
- Sessions are typically 60-75 minutes
- Usually scheduled weekly or bi-weekly
- Both partners attend together (individual sessions may be incorporated as needed)
- Telehealth options available for couples

========================================
THERAPEUTIC APPROACHES
========================================

Our therapists are trained in multiple evidence-based modalities and tailor treatment to each client:

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT):
- Focuses on identifying and changing unhelpful thought patterns and behaviors
- Highly effective for anxiety, depression, and many other concerns
- Structured, goal-oriented approach
- Includes homework and skills practice between sessions

Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT):
- Originally developed for emotion regulation difficulties
- Teaches mindfulness, distress tolerance, emotion regulation, and interpersonal effectiveness
- Particularly helpful for intense emotions and relationship challenges
- Combines individual therapy with skills training

Emotionally Focused Therapy (EFT):
- Attachment-based approach, particularly effective for couples
- Helps identify and transform emotional patterns
- Creates secure emotional bonds
- Evidence-based with strong research support

Mindfulness-Based Approaches:
- Incorporates meditation and present-moment awareness
- Reduces stress and increases emotional regulation
- Can be integrated with other therapeutic approaches
- Helps develop greater self-awareness and acceptance

========================================
WHAT TO EXPECT
========================================

Initial Consultation:
- First session focuses on understanding your concerns and goals
- We'll discuss your history and current situation
- Therapist will explain their approach and answer questions
- Together, you'll determine if it's a good fit
- No commitment required after the first session

Ongoing Therapy:
- Collaborative goal-setting
- Regular sessions (typically weekly)
- Skills and strategies to use between sessions
- Periodic review of progress
- Flexible treatment length based on your needs

Treatment Duration:
- Some clients benefit from short-term focused work (8-12 sessions)
- Others engage in longer-term therapy for deeper work
- Duration depends on concerns, goals, and individual preferences
- You and your therapist will discuss and adjust as needed

========================================
TELEHEALTH SERVICES
========================================

We offer secure, HIPAA-compliant video therapy sessions for clients who prefer remote options or cannot attend in person.

Benefits of Telehealth:
- Convenience and flexibility
- No commute time
- Access from home or private location
- Equally effective for many concerns
- Continuity of care when traveling or relocating

Requirements:
- Private, quiet space for sessions
- Reliable internet connection
- Computer, tablet, or smartphone with camera and microphone
- We use secure, encrypted video platform

========================================
GETTING STARTED
========================================

Taking the first step toward therapy can feel challenging, but we're here to make it as comfortable as possible.

How to Begin:
1. Contact Psyling by phone or email
2. Brief phone consultation to discuss your needs
3. Schedule your initial appointment
4. Complete intake paperwork (sent electronically before first session)
5. Attend your first session

We understand that reaching out takes courage, and our staff is here to support you through the process.
