UNDERSTANDING MENTAL HEALTH PROFESSIONALS
========================================

What's the difference between a psychologist, psychiatrist, and psychotherapist?

Psychiatrists:
- Medical doctors (MD)
- Can make diagnoses
- Can prescribe medication
- Sometimes provide therapy

Psychologists:
- Conduct assessments and psychological testing
- Sometimes provide therapy
- Cannot prescribe medication in Canada

Psychotherapists (like Valery Belyanin):
- Provide various kinds of psychotherapy
- Are not medical doctors
- Cannot prescribe medication in Canada
- Cannot communicate diagnoses in Ontario
- Registered with professional colleges (CRPO in Ontario)

Do psychotherapists prescribe medications?
No. Neither psychologists nor psychotherapists prescribe medications in Canada.
