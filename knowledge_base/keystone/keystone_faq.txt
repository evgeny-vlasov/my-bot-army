KEYSTONE HARDSCAPES - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

GENERAL QUESTIONS

Q: How long does a typical interlock installation take?
A: Most residential driveways take 3-5 days. Patios typically take 2-4 days. Larger or complex projects may take 1-2 weeks. Timeline depends on project size, pattern complexity, and weather conditions.

Q: What is the best time of year for installation?
A: May through October is ideal in Calgary. We need temperatures above 5°C for proper installation. Spring and fall are often less busy, potentially offering better scheduling flexibility.

Q: Do you provide free estimates?
A: Yes, we provide free on-site consultations and detailed written estimates for all projects.

Q: How long will my interlock last?
A: With proper installation and maintenance, quality interlock can last 25-30 years or more. Our workmanship warranty covers 5 years.

INSTALLATION PROCESS

Q: Do I need to remove my existing driveway/patio?
A: Yes, we typically remove existing surfaces to ensure proper base preparation. Proper base is critical for long-term performance.

Q: Will you handle permits?
A: We can advise on permit requirements. In Calgary, most residential interlock projects don't require permits, but we'll confirm based on your specific project.

Q: What happens if it rains during installation?
A: Light rain usually isn't a problem, but heavy rain may delay work. We'll protect your property and resume when conditions are suitable.

Q: How much disruption should I expect?
A: There will be noise from equipment and temporary loss of access to the work area. We work efficiently to minimize inconvenience and clean up daily.

MATERIALS AND DESIGN

Q: What materials do you recommend?
A: We work with top Canadian manufacturers. Material choice depends on your project type, budget, and aesthetic preferences. We'll guide you through options during consultation.

Q: Can I see samples before deciding?
A: Absolutely! We can show you physical samples and photos of completed projects with similar materials and patterns.

Q: Can you match existing interlock for additions?
A: We'll do our best to match or complement existing work. Exact matches can be challenging due to weathering and discontinued products, but we find solutions that blend well.

Q: What colors are available?
A: Dozens of colors from charcoal and grey tones to earth tones, reds, and specialty colors. We'll help you choose colors that complement your home.

MAINTENANCE

Q: How do I maintain my interlock?
A: Regular sweeping, occasional pressure washing (carefully), and polymeric sand replenishment every few years. We can provide detailed maintenance instructions and offer maintenance services.

Q: Should I seal my interlock?
A: Sealing is optional but recommended. It protects against stains, enhances color, and inhibits weed growth. We offer sealing services at installation or later.

Q: What if pavers become unlevel over time?
A: Minor settling can occur. If covered under warranty, we'll repair it. Otherwise, re-leveling is a straightforward repair service we offer.

Q: How do I remove stains?
A: Most stains can be removed with appropriate cleaners. We can advise on specific stain types or provide cleaning services.

Q: Do you offer financing?
A: Please inquire about current financing options during your consultation.

Q: What's included in the quote?
A: Our quotes include all labor, materials, base preparation, and cleanup. Any exclusions will be clearly noted.

Q: Do you require a deposit?
A: Yes, we typically require a deposit to schedule your project and order materials. Final payment is due upon completion.

WINTER AND SEASONAL

Q: Can interlock handle Calgary winters?
A: Yes! Quality interlock is designed for freeze-thaw cycles. Proper installation with adequate base and drainage is key to winter performance.

Q: Should I use ice melt on interlock?
A: Avoid sodium chloride (rock salt) which can damage pavers and vegetation. Use calcium chloride or sand. We can recommend safe de-icing products.

Q: Can I plow or snow blow my interlock driveway?
A: Yes, but use care. Adjust blade height to avoid catching on pavers. Rubber-edged blades are ideal.

REPAIRS

Q: Do you repair other companies' work?
A: Yes, we repair and restore interlock installations regardless of who did the original work.

Q: Can individual pavers be replaced?
A: Yes, individual damaged pavers can be removed and replaced. Color matching depends on weathering and product availability.

WARRANTY

Q: What does the warranty cover?
A: Our 5-year workmanship warranty covers settling, installation defects, and structural issues. Normal wear, damage from improper maintenance, or Acts of God are not covered.

Q: What about material warranty?
A: Materials carry manufacturer warranties (typically 25+ years). We'll provide warranty information for your chosen products.
