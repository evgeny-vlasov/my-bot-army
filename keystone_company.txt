KEYSTONE HARDSCAPES - COMPANY INFORMATION

ABOUT US
Keystone Hardscapes is a Calgary-based hardscaping company specializing in premium interlock installation, retaining walls, patios, and driveways. We serve residential and commercial clients throughout Calgary and surrounding areas.

OUR EXPERTISE
- Interlock driveways and patios
- Retaining walls and garden walls
- Outdoor living spaces
- Hardscape repairs and restoration
- Custom design and installation

WHAT SETS US APART
Quality Craftsmanship: Every project receives meticulous attention to detail from experienced installers who understand Calgary's climate requirements.

Proper Installation: We don't cut corners on base preparation. A quality base is the foundation of a long-lasting installation.

Local Knowledge: We understand Calgary's unique challenges - freeze-thaw cycles, clay soils, and drainage requirements.

Customer Service: Clear communication, realistic timelines, and professional conduct throughout your project.

CALGARY-SPECIFIC CONSIDERATIONS
We design and install hardscaping specifically for Calgary's climate:
- Freeze-thaw resistant materials
- Proper drainage for spring runoff
- Base preparation for clay soils
- Installation timing for optimal results
